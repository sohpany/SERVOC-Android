## Yemen-specific external studies relevant to environmental / occupational measurements

Candidate studies identified for P1/P2 verification:

- *Unveiling air pollution patterns in Yemen: a spatial–temporal functional data analysis* — PM2.5, O3, CO and SO2 across 37 Yemeni cities (1980–2022). Useful for national air-pollution context.

- *Indoor/Outdoor Air Quality and Children Health Risk Assessment for PM10 and PM2.5 on Urban Areas at Al-Mukalla City, Yemen* — direct particulate measurements inside/outside homes using air sampling. Useful as an example of objective environmental measurement in Yemen.

- *Activity concentration of indoor radon and its short-lived progeny ... in Sana'a, Yemen* — direct indoor contaminant measurement using electronic detectors/air monitors. Useful for the broader environmental-monitoring context, not as evidence for HazRob gas-identification performance.

- *Study of Environmental Pollution Resulting from Balhaf Liquid Natural Gas Station Using Spectroscopy Analysis, Shabwah Governorate – Yemen* — soil contamination / spectroscopy in an industrial-emissions context.

- *The Effect of Occupational Benzene Exposure Duration on Hematological Parameters among Fuel Station Workers in Mukalla City, Yemen* — occupational chemical-exposure context; not a HazRob performance source.

- *Unintentional Injuries in the Three Reference Laboratories: Sana'a, Yemen* — reported injury patterns including chemical exposure among laboratory staff; useful for contextual OSH burden only.
