## Sending and evidence rule

No row may be marked SENT without authenticated mail-tool evidence. The exact recipient, message identifier, request scope and any response are recorded here. All requests state that HazRob will cite the dataset/publication and authors in research outputs and will obey any supplied license/redistribution restriction. External data may be used as `RESEARCH_EXTERNAL` before HR069 transfer validation, but no external source is promoted to device-specific M4 by correspondence or publication alone.
